06 — Section Strategy
Background Rhythm & Section Spacing

The website alternates between light, off-white, and dark sections to create visual rhythm. No two consecutive sections feel identical in visual weight.

Light — Warm White #FAFAF8
Primary content area. Premium, spacious, breathing room.

Used for: Hero (light variant), About, Case studies, Blog, Team.

Alt — Off-White #F2F1EE
Subtle variation without heavy contrast.

Used for: Service grids, feature lists, process steps, FAQ sections.

Dark — Near Black #181818
High-impact sections. Authority and confidence.

Used for: Hero (dark), CTA sections, stats, bold feature moments.

Dark Charcoal — #222222
Softer dark. Warm and premium.

Used for: Footer, testimonials (dark), partner sections.

13 — Motion & Animation
Interaction Principles

Motion is purposeful, never decorative. Transitions communicate state changes; hover lifts signal interactivity. No idle looping animations. Hover the cards to preview each principle.

Animation Direction Summary

Scroll entrance: fade-up • 500ms • 80ms stagger
Hover: 150–250ms ease-in-out
Active/press: scale(0.97) • 150ms
Page transitions: fade • 300ms
Number counters: count-up on scroll • 1200ms
Avoid: idle looping, flashy entrances

Easing Reference

Default: cubic-bezier(.25,.46,.45,.94)
Spring: cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)
Ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)
Fast: 150ms • Normal: 250ms • Slow: 400ms
